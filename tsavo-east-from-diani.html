Tsavo East from Diani

Private 4×4 safari to Kenya’s red-elephant country — depart from your Diani or Mombasa hotel, no domestic flight required

Book via WhatsApp Open Full Package

Tsavo East is the natural short safari for guests already on the Kenyan coast. An early road departure from Diani or Mombasa puts you in the park for afternoon game drives; lodge nights and private Landcruiser keep the trip focused on wildlife rather than airports.

Highlights

  • Famous red elephants along the Galana and open plains
  • Private 4×4 with professional guide — your pace, your stops
  • Full-board lodge stays on standard packages
  • Easy to combine with a Wasini marine day the same week

Typical 3-day outline

Day 1 — Coast pickup, drive to Tsavo East, lunch, afternoon game drive, lodge night.
Day 2 — Full day exploring riverine and open habitats.
Day 3 — Morning drive, return to Diani or Mombasa.

2-day and longer options (including links toward Amboseli) are available on request.
