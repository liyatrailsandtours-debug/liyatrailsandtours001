Amboseli Safari from Mombasa

Private 4×4 expedition to elephant country under Kilimanjaro — organised from Mombasa, Diani and the Kenyan coast

Book via WhatsApp Open Full Package

Amboseli is world-famous for large elephant herds and clear views of Mount Kilimanjaro on good weather days. From the coast we arrange private road or road-plus-air combinations so you can start in Mombasa or Diani and finish with swamp-edge game drives and lodge nights in the park.

Why book from the coast with us

  • One concierge for beach hotels and inland safari logistics
  • Private vehicle and guide — not a fixed bus-tour schedule
  • Clear inclusions: park fees, full board, transfers as agreed
  • Easy to pair with Wasini, fishing or a Tsavo East add-on

Sample 3-day shape

Day 1 — Depart coast (or Nairobi link), arrive Amboseli, afternoon game drive.
Day 2 — Full day among swamps and plains; Observation Hill viewpoints when suitable.
Day 3 — Morning wildlife, return toward the coast or onward connection.

Longer circuits (Tsavo + Amboseli, or Amboseli + Mara) are designed around your dates.
