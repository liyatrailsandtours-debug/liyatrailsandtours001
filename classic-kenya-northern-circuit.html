Tailor-Made Kenya Safari

Classic Kenya Northern Circuit | Tailor-Made Kenya Safari | Liya Trails & Tours

The complete classic inland itinerary — Mara predators and migration, Nakuru’s rhinos and flamingos, and the unique northern species of Samburu. Pure wildlife, no coast required.

Duration7 days / 6 nights
DestinationMaasai Mara, Lake Nakuru, Samburu
Travel styleFully tailor-made
Starting price$2,431.31 per person

Detailed day-by-day itinerary

This itinerary is designed as a tailor-made journey. Exact departure times, park routing, meal arrangements and activities can be refined around your dates and accommodation confirmation.

Day 1: Nairobi – Maasai Mara National Reserve

Depart Nairobi after breakfast and travel to the Maasai Mara. Arrive for lunch and an afternoon game drive before dinner.

Accommodation: Kambu Mara

Day 2: Full Day Maasai Mara

Enjoy a full day of game drives across the Mara plains, with opportunities for big cats, elephants, buffalo, giraffes and other wildlife.

Accommodation: Kambu Mara

Day 3: Maasai Mara – Lake Nakuru / Rift Valley

Morning game drive and breakfast, followed by departure towards the Rift Valley. Continue to the Lake Nakuru area for an afternoon wildlife experience where timing permits.

Accommodation: Dove Nest Lodge Naivasha

Day 4: Lake Nakuru – Samburu National Reserve

After breakfast, continue north towards Samburu. Lunch is arranged en route, followed by an arrival game drive where time permits.

Accommodation: Samburu Simba Lodge

Day 5: Full Day Samburu

Explore Samburu National Reserve with morning and afternoon game drives, focusing on the reserve’s distinctive northern species and riverine wildlife.

Accommodation: Samburu Simba Lodge

Day 6: Samburu Continued

Enjoy another full day in Samburu with flexible game-drive routing based on wildlife movement and the group’s interests. Return to the lodge for dinner and overnight stay.

Accommodation: Samburu Simba Lodge

Day 7: Final Morning & Return to Nairobi

Take a final sunrise game drive, breakfast and check out before travelling back to Nairobi.
