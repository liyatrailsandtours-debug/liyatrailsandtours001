Wasini Island Day Trip

Dolphin watching · Kisite Mpunguti snorkeling · Dhow & Swahili seafood lunch — from Diani, Ukunda and Shimoni

Book via WhatsApp All Tours

The Wasini Island day trip is the signature marine experience from Kenya’s south coast. You leave the beach hotels early, board at Shimoni, watch for wild dolphins on the way into Kisite Mpunguti Marine Park, snorkel on protected reefs, then settle into a leisurely seafood lunch on Wasini before returning in the afternoon.

Dhow & motorboat · Kisite reefs · Wasini Island lunch

Who this is for

Couples, families and small groups staying in Diani, Ukunda, Tiwi or near Shimoni who want a full ocean day without overnight luggage. Private charters suit those who want flexible timing; shared departures suit travellers happy to join a small group.

What’s typically included

Included Hotel transfers (south coast) · Boat & crew · Park fees · Snorkeling gear · Wasini lunch · Guide coordination
Usually excluded Alcoholic drinks · Personal tips · Optional crafts · Travel insurance

How to book

Message us on WhatsApp with your hotel, preferred date and group size. We confirm availability, send a clear quote, and hold the boat once a deposit is received. Same-week requests are often possible outside peak holidays.
