Tracking Elephants in Tsavo East

Red dust · Galana River · Private 4×4 from the coast

Tsavo East is Kenya’s classic “big sky” park — open plains, the Galana River corridor, and the famous red elephants dusted by the iron-rich soil. For guests based in Diani or Mombasa, it is the most natural short safari: drive in, game drive, lodge nights, drive back — no domestic flight required for a 2–3 day trip.

Why it works from the coast

Road distance from the south coast is manageable with an early start. You spend your time in the park rather than airports. Private Landcruiser means your pace, your photo stops, and no shared-vehicle schedule.

What we usually include

  • Hotel pickup and drop-off on the coast
  • Park entry fees and private 4×4 with guide
  • Full-board lodge nights (e.g. Ashnil Aruba or similar on standard packages)
  • Game drives focused on elephants, predators, and riverine habitat

Sample rhythm

Day 1: Depart coast, arrive for lunch, afternoon game drive.
Day 2: Full day in the park — Galana, open plains, optional Lugard Falls viewpoint depending on access and season.
Day 3: Morning drive, return to Diani or Mombasa.

Longer circuits can add Taita Hills or continue to Amboseli for Kilimanjaro views.

Best combined with

A Wasini Island marine day before or after — beach and bush in one week without rushing. See our Tsavo East from Diani page for package entry points.
