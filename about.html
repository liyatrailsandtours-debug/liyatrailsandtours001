Our story

Born on the coast. Built for the whole of Kenya.

Liya Trails & Tours brings together the coast we know intimately and the classic safari country that makes Kenya extraordinary.

Our approach is simple: listen first, plan carefully, and keep the journey coherent from the first transfer to the final day. Beach, marine experiences, wildlife and culture can sit within one thoughtfully designed itinerary.

We care about the details that make travel feel effortless: realistic routing, dependable logistics, knowledgeable guides and communication when you need it.
