About Liya Trails

Coast-based · Wildlife-led · Built around you

Our Story

Liya Trails and Tours is a premier luxury tour operator based on Kenya’s coastal region. We design exceptional travel experiences across East Africa — from private marine excursions and island days to multi-day wildlife safaris in Tsavo, Amboseli, and the Masai Mara.

We live and work on the Indian Ocean shore. That proximity means intimate knowledge of marine parks, dhow routes, seasonal dolphin activity, and the best windows for connecting beach time with inland game drives. Every itinerary is built around your pace, interests, and comfort — never a fixed bus-tour template.
